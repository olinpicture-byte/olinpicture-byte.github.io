Privacy Policy (Mimicco Uploader)

Last updated: 2026-11-24

What this app is

"Mimicco Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Mimicco YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

Google user data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload Mimicco's own videos (Shorts and weekly recaps) to the Mimicco channel.
  • YouTube management (youtube.force-ssl): to find and prepare Mimicco's own live streams and read their public live chat so Mimicco can respond to viewers.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • Live chat messages (public chat of Mimicco's own streams) are used to let Mimicco reply on stream and may be kept in local logs on that computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
